  8 FAIL d e

== causal_chain
input: x 1 2 3 4
property: interesting iff it contains x and its digits are exactly 1..k, so digit k is removable only once digit k+1 is gone
  1 FAIL (empty)
  2 FAIL 2 3 4
  3 PASS x 1
  4 FAIL 1
  5 PASS x
result: x
