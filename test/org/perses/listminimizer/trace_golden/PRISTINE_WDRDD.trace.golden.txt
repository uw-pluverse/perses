  3 PASS a b e

  5 FAIL b e
  6 PASS a e

  9 FAIL a

== causal_chain
input: x 1 2 3 4
property: interesting iff it contains x and its digits are exactly 1..k, so digit k is removable only once digit k+1 is gone
  1 FAIL (empty)
  2 FAIL 2 3 4
  3 FAIL x 1 4
  4 PASS x 1 2 3
  5 FAIL 1 2 3
  6 FAIL x 2 3
  7 FAIL x 1 3
  8 PASS x 1 2
  9 FAIL 1 2
 10 FAIL x 2
 11 PASS x 1
 12 FAIL 1
 13 PASS x
result: x
